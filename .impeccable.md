## Design Context

### Users

Lumino primarily serves ordinary business, foreign-trade, customs, procurement, production, finance and EHS staff at Chinese and other non-EU exporters and manufacturers. They need to produce reliable CBAM information for importers and verifiers, but should not be expected to understand regulatory field names or learn the Communication Form before they can use the product.

The primary job is to provide business facts and existing evidence, confirm what the system has understood, delegate questions to the right colleagues or suppliers, and receive traceable compliance deliverables. Lumino experts and verifiers need a separate professional view over the same underlying data. Operations and commercial staff need progress and relationship views without default access to sensitive production evidence. The same enterprise may have several annual compliance cycles active at once, including current-year collection and prior-year verification or correction.

### Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy and quietly rigorous. The product should feel like a capable compliance colleague: direct without being abrupt, patient without being verbose, and careful without making routine work feel intimidating.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light, workday interface with an editorial-operational character rather than a conventional enterprise form system. Lumino's deep green, warm yellow and lightly green-tinted neutrals remain the recognizable palette. The Agent workspace should prioritize one understandable task at a time, with compliance detail progressively disclosed instead of presented as dense forms.

Avoid chatbot theatre, generic card dashboards, excessive professional terminology, dark AI-console styling, decorative gradients, and interfaces that resemble a digitized Communication Form.

### Design Principles

1. Ask for observable business facts, not regulatory fields.
2. Prefer confirming, uploading or delegating over retyping.
3. Reveal one decision at a time and always provide an “I don't know” path.
4. Keep Agent proposals visibly separate from confirmed compliance data.
5. Every formal result must remain traceable to evidence, rules, confirmation and version history.
6. Customer mode and expert mode share one source of truth but expose different levels of detail.
7. Keep one coordinating Agent visible to users while specialist Agents work behind explicit scopes.
8. Make the active reporting year unmistakable and treat locked years as immutable snapshots.
9. Shape every screen and Agent answer by role, object scope and data sensitivity—not by role name alone.
